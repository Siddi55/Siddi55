Role

Content, SEO & Site Management

The brief

The DermaDraft exists to give people research-backed, jargon-free skincare information — no affiliate-driven hype, no vague advice. I run every part of it, alone: from what gets written to how fast the page loads.

What I did

Content Strategy & Writing

Topic research, outlines, and full articles — written for readers first, structured so search engines can understand them too.

On-Page SEO

Keyword mapping, title and meta optimization, header structure, internal linking between related articles.

Technical SEO

Site speed checks, indexing issues, sitemap and Search Console monitoring, fixing crawl errors as they appear.

WordPress Management

Theme setup, plugin maintenance, page structure, day-to-day upkeep with no dev team behind me.

Visual Design

Featured images, in-article graphics, and a consistent visual style — all built in Canva.

Results

94 clicks and 20.2k impressions over the last 3 months, tracked in Google Search Console — and growing.
